USABILITY TEST – REVIEW / Q&A FEATURE

-----------------------------------
Intro
-----------------------------------
We are a team building an online hat store. The feature being tested allows users to view reviews, ask questions, and leave feedback on products.

The participant will act as a customer interested in purchasing a hat. They should think out loud while interacting with the prototype and explain their thoughts before taking actions.

-----------------------------------
Tasks
-----------------------------------
Task 1:
You are interested in buying a hat but want to learn more about it before making a decision. Try to find information from other customers about the product.

Task 2:
You have a question about the product regarding returns or exchanges. Try to find where you would ask this question and how you would do it.

Task 3:
You have already purchased the product and want to share your experience. Try to leave feedback for others.

-----------------------------------
Notes
-----------------------------------

Participant 1:
- Looked for guidance on hat sizing and how it would fit their head
- Asked about returns, refunds, and exchange process
- Focused on scrolling through reviews to decide if they were good or bad
- Expected basic product questions to already be answered

Participant 2:
- Noticed difference between verified and non-verified users
- Liked filtering options but wanted more specific filtering
- Asked for more detailed fit information (especially for head shape)
- Suggested having a size chart or clearer description

-----------------------------------
Feedback
-----------------------------------

Participant 1:
- Wanted a clearer process for returns and exchanges
- Expected answers to common questions without needing to ask
- Suggested adding more structured product-related information

Participant 2:
- Found filters helpful but wanted more specific categories
- Said knowing if a reviewer is verified is important for trust
- Wanted better guidance on choosing the right size

-----------------------------------
Results
-----------------------------------

Issue 1: Lack of sizing guidance
- Users were unsure how to choose the correct hat size
- Hypothesis: The product page does not provide enough fit-related information
- Remedy: Add a size chart or model that helps relate hat size to head shape

Issue 2: Confusion around Q&A vs Reviews
- Users were unsure where to ask questions or leave feedback
- Quote: “I don’t know if I should ask here or leave a review”
- Hypothesis: The label “Q&A” is unclear
- Remedy: Rename section to “Help / Leave a Review”

Issue 3: Lack of trust in reviews
- Users cared about whether reviews were from verified buyers
- Quote: “I would trust this more if I knew they bought it”
- Hypothesis: No distinction reduces credibility
- Remedy: Add verified vs non-verified labels

Issue 4: Need for better interaction
- Users wanted responses to questions
- Quote: “It would be better if the company could reply under this”
- Hypothesis: Static reviews limit engagement
- Remedy: Implement chat/thread-style responses under questions

-----------------------------------
Updates (Based on Feedback)
-----------------------------------
- Added size chart / sizing guidance for hats
- Renamed Q&A section to “Help / Leave a Review”
- Planned verified customer labels for reviews
- Designed chat-style reply system for questions
